SigLIP 2 (siglip2-base-patch16-224)
===================================

Sanctuary Toolkit's "Recognise items by their icon" uses the vision half of SigLIP 2 to turn a
picture into a vector. The model and its weights are not ours.

Model:      google/siglip2-base-patch16-224
            https://huggingface.co/google/siglip2-base-patch16-224
Copyright:  Google LLC and the SigLIP 2 authors
Licence:    Apache License 2.0 (see siglip2-LICENSE-Apache-2.0.txt)
Paper:      "SigLIP 2: Multilingual Vision-Language Encoders with Improved Semantic
            Understanding, Localization, and Dense Features", Tschannen et al., 2025
            https://arxiv.org/abs/2502.14786

ONNX export: onnx-community/siglip2-base-patch16-224-ONNX
             https://huggingface.co/onnx-community/siglip2-base-patch16-224-ONNX
             An unmodified ONNX export of the model above, by the onnx-community
             organisation on Hugging Face. The export repository states no licence of its
             own; the weights it contains stay under the Apache 2.0 licence above.

What Sanctuary Toolkit ships: nothing of the model. The release asset holding the ONNX vision
encoder is downloaded once, from this repository's own GitHub release, when you turn icon
recognition on, and is kept in the app's cache folder. This NOTICE and the licence text travel
with it.

Runtime:    ONNX Runtime Web (onnxruntime-web), Copyright (c) Microsoft Corporation,
            MIT licence. See node_modules/onnxruntime-web/LICENSE in the source tree.
